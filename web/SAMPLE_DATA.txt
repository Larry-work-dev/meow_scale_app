/*
 * Each weight reading has:
 * - Base weight value (grams)
 * - Small noise/jitter (±0.1-0.2g) due to sensor sensitivity
 * - Smoothing will reduce this noise on client side
 * 
 * CSV lines end with \n (newline character)
 * 
 * Flow rate is calculated on client:
 *   flow_rate = (current_weight - previous_weight) / time_delta
 * 
 * Normal values:
 * - Tare: ~0 ±0.2g
 * - Bloom pour: 2-5 g/s
 * - Regular pour: 5-10 g/s
 * - End of pour: 0.1-1 g/s
 * 
 * Alert triggers:
 * - First alert: 30 seconds from start
 * - Subsequent alerts: every 60 seconds (60s, 120s, 180s, etc.)
 */
